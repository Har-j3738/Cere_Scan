# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CereScan is a student research project. We welcome respectful collaboration from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Explain reproducible technical problems clearly.
- Give credit to dataset authors, libraries, and contributors.
- Do not publish private information or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, or personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from the project.

For concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub.
